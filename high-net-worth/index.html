03
Trusts, Estate & Business Succession

Where income tax and estate tax meet

Every trust structure moves income, control, and your taxable estate differently — and how much of your lifetime estate and gift tax exemption you use, and when, shapes the whole plan. Getting the structure right matters as much as the number on this year's return.

{% include icons/shield-check.html %}

Grantor Trusts

Income is taxed to you, the grantor, rather than the trust — flexible, and often a deliberate first step in a longer-term estate plan.

{% include icons/shield-check.html %}

Irrevocable Trusts

Once assets move in, they're generally out of your taxable estate — a real tradeoff of control for a real reduction in future estate tax exposure.

{% include icons/heart-handshake.html %}

Charitable Remainder Trusts

An income stream for you now, a charitable deduction today, and the remainder to charity later — one structure doing three jobs at once.

{% include icons/users.html %}

Business Succession Planning

Family business or key employees taking over one day? We help structure the exit — timing, valuation, and tax treatment — well before the day it actually happens.

How income flows through each of these to your personal return — and how that interacts with estate taxes down the line — is where the real planning happens. That's not a decision to make from a blog post.
