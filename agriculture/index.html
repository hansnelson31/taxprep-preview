03
How We Think

We dig into details

The best outcome usually hides in the details. Before we file, we optimize your tax bill by taking advantage of every strategy that fits your operation.

  • Bonus depreciation & asset-life elections — so that taxable income hits the sweet spot.
  • Crop-insurance income deferrals — flexibility in recognizing crop insurance income by pushing it to the next year.
  • 1031 exchanges & monetized installment sales — defer gains as you swap ground or exit the business.
  • Conservation-easement deductions & credits — gain tax savings while guaranteeing the farm to future generations.
  • R&D tax credits — for the test plots and feed rations you already run.
  • Section 180 excess-nutrient deductions — writing off the residual soil fertility in newly purchased ground.

…are but a few of the many areas we look at to optimize your tax situation.
