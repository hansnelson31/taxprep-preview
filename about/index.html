Why It Matters

Why work with a CPA?

{% include icons/shield-check.html %}

Training

CPAs complete 150-plus college credit hours, pass a rigorous multi-part exam, and then keep at least 40 hours of continuing education every year just to stay licensed. It doesn't let up after the test.

{% include icons/shield-check.html %}

Representation Authority

CPAs are among the few professionals allowed to represent clients directly before the IRS and US Tax Court, without needing a law degree or bar admission — real authority if a return is ever questioned.

{% include icons/shield-check.html %}

Communication

Numbers only matter if you understand them. CPAs translate complex financial information into language that actually makes sense — for you, your lender, your partners, or anyone else who needs the real picture.

{% include icons/shield-check.html %}

Ethical Standards

Honesty: truthful, accurate information. Integrity: ethical guidelines that prioritize your interest over convenience. Objectivity: independent judgment, free from conflicts of interest or pressure to tell you what you want to hear.
