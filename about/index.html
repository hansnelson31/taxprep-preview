Why It Matters

Why work with a CPA?

Training

CPAs complete 150+ college credits, pass a rigorous multi-part exam, and then maintain 40 hours of continuing education every year to keep their license.

Representation Authority

CPAs are one of the few professionals allowed to represent clients before the US Tax Court, without needing to be admitted to the bar.

Communication

CPAs translate complex financial information into language that actually makes sense — for you, your lender, or anyone else who needs to understand your numbers.

Ethical Standards

Honesty: truthful, accurate, unbiased information. Integrity: adherence to ethical guidelines that prioritize the public interest. Objectivity: independent judgment, free from personal bias or conflicts of interest.
