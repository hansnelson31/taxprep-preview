Privacy Policy — QBO Integration

Last updated: July 29, 2026

This application is a private, internal tool used solely by HGN Tax Prep to access QuickBooks Online data for our own accounting and tax preparation workflows. It is not distributed to the public and does not collect, store, or share data with any third party beyond what is necessary to retrieve information directly from Intuit's QuickBooks Online API for internal use.

Data accessed through this application is used only for tax preparation and bookkeeping services provided by HGN Tax Prep, and is handled in accordance with our firm's client confidentiality obligations.
